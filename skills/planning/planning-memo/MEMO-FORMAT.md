# planning-memo.md Format

## Structure

```markdown
# Planning Memo: <Engagement name>

## Engagement context

> <Audit objective statement, unchanged from ENGAGEMENT.md>

<Assurance or advisory, with its basis. Links to the survey and assessment
with their versions, and the methodology or default basis.>

## Engagement objectives

1. <Objective wording. Linked Objective IDs and Risk IDs; mandated source
   where applicable; auditor's agreement with date and reason.>

## Scope

<Period under examination, entities and locations, and systems.>

| Process ID | Process Title | Scoping considerations | In/Out | Reason | Date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Risk ID | Risk Title | Process IDs | Band | Scoping considerations | In/Out | Reason | Date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Exclusions and scope limitations

| Excluded item | Affected references | Basis | Reason | Management discussion and escalation | Date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Criteria

| Engagement objective | Criteria | Source | Type | Adequacy judgement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Topical requirements applicability

<Sourced list, considerations, and auditor decisions with dates, or the
one-line basis for no list.>

## Approach

<Summary per engagement objective, including any reliance on other providers'
work with its competence, objectivity, and due care basis.>

## Resources

| Name | Role | Objectivity confirmation | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

<Supervisor. Total budget and hours by phase, and milestones, with sources and
any inconsistency. Sufficiency considerations, the auditor's judgement, and
any escalation.>

## Information gaps

<Affected section, whether the gap could change scope or objectives, and the
returned RQ ID or question put to the auditor. Include outstanding
methodology requirements and handoffs.>

## Sources

<Retained copies used and other stated bases, preserving provenance and
prior-content labels.>
```

## Use of the format

- Keep sections in this order, retaining missing content with bracketed gaps.
- Order the risk scoping table in the assessment's ranked order.
- Mark each exclusion's Basis as auditor's judgement or scope limitation. List risk exclusions first in the assessment's ranked order, then other exclusions; this preserves priority when methodology uses different band names.
- Mark each criterion's Type as management, external, framework, or auditor-developed.
- Keep superseded scoping decisions visible as history below the current entries.
