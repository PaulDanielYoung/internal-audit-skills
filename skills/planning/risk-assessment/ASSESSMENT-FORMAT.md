# risk-assessment.md Format

## Structure

```markdown
# Risk Assessment: <Engagement name>

## Engagement context

> <Audit objective statement, unchanged from ENGAGEMENT.md>

<Link to the survey, or the provisional entry basis and process list. The
internal audit plan's risk rationale and changes since the plan was approved,
carried from the survey with sources.>

## Activity objectives

| Objective ID | Objective | Performance measures and targets | Sources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

<Management's established risk tolerance with its source, or the stated
absence of one.>

## Rating method

<Scale, calibration if supplied, formula, bands, and methodology or default
basis.>

## Risk summary

| Risk ID | Risk Title | Process IDs | Objective IDs | Likelihood | Impact | Score | Band |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

<Heat map of rated Risk IDs: one row per likelihood value, highest first, and
one column per impact value, lowest first, sized to the rating scale. List
unrated Risk IDs below it.>

## Risks

### <Risk ID>: <Risk Title>

<Description, basis, linked Process IDs and Objective IDs, likelihood
considerations, impact considerations, and the recorded rating with date and
reason. Include wording-review status, changes, and flags where applicable.>

<Retirement history below the active risks.>

## Fraud consideration

| Fraud scheme | Risk IDs or auditor's reason none is retained | Date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Misappropriation of assets | | |
| Corruption | | |
| Fraudulent reporting | | |
| Management override of controls | | |

<The survey's fraud indicators considered, with sources.>

## Coverage check

<Topics considered, linked risks or sourced reasons none applies, and
outstanding questions.>

## Management's view

<Management risk sources compared, with dates. Management-identified risks
not retained, with reasons; retained risks management has not identified. State
when management's view was unavailable.>

## Information gaps

<Affected risk or section, whether the gap could add, remove, or re-rate a
risk, and its returned RQ ID or question put to the auditor. Include
outstanding methodology requirements and unresolved handoffs.>

## Sources

<Retained copies used and other stated bases, preserving provenance and
prior-content labels.>
```

## Use of the format

- Keep sections in this order, retaining missing content with bracketed gaps.
- Order the risk summary and risk entries by the same ranking.
- Generate the summary's Score and Band and the heat map from the same computed results.
