## Internal audit skills

`GLOSSARY.md`, `METHODOLOGY.md`, and `ORGANIZATION.md` sit beside this file. Use these copies even when working inside `engagements/`.

Before making audit judgments or producing audit work, read the glossary and the relevant parts of the methodology and organization files. Use the glossary's terms, follow documented methodology and its cited sources, and ask about material gaps where these files are silent.

When a term is missing, contested, or settled during audit work, or a methodology point or organizational fact useful across engagements comes up, propose recording it with `audit-context` before moving on. Only that skill edits these three files. Keep engagement scope, evidence, and outputs under `engagements/`.

Each engagement lives in `engagements/<year>/<name>/` and is identified by its `ENGAGEMENT.md`, which `create-engagement` creates. Shared-context maintenance, engagement creation, and work across engagements need no engagement selection. Before producing work for one engagement, the auditor names it by name or folder in the conversation: resolve that to one engagement folder, asking when it is ambiguous, read its `ENGAGEMENT.md`, and state the selected engagement and output path. Keep that selection for the rest of the conversation. When none has been established, ask rather than inferring one from the last file encountered. Reading another engagement as background does not change the selection; recommend a fresh conversation for work on another engagement.
