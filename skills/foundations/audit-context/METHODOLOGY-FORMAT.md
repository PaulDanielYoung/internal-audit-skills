# METHODOLOGY.md Format

## Structure

```md
# Audit Methodology

The organization's documented audit methodology, converted from its audit manual and other governing sources.

## {Methodology area}

{A concise account of the documented requirements, preserving required versus recommended steps, applicability conditions, scales, thresholds, and exceptions.}

Source: [{Document title}]({path-or-url}), {section or page}, {version or date when available}.
```

## Rules

- **Follow documented methodology.** Documented methodology governs audit work. The source governs if the summary differs; an informal practice or preference does not amend it.
- **Keep decision rules intact.** Preserve required versus recommended steps, applicability conditions, scales, thresholds, and exceptions when summarizing.
- **Link requirements to sources.** Link each requirement or coherent group to the actual source title and accessible path or URL, with its section or page and version or date when available.
- **Keep the account concise.** Reference lengthy procedures or templates and state when a consuming skill must consult them.
- **Organize around the supplied methodology.** Add headings for supported areas, prioritizing those needed for current work, such as engagement planning, risk assessment, scope justification, required deliverables, and review or approval.
- **Keep gaps distinct from requirements.** A missing rule is not evidence that no requirement applies. Record requirements supported by governing sources rather than filling gaps with generic audit practice.
