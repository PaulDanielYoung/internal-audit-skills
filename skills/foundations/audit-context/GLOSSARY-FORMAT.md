# GLOSSARY.md Format

## Structure

```md
# Glossary

The organization's own audit vocabulary: each term's agreed meaning and the synonyms it displaces. Every skill reads it; only `audit-context` edits it.

## Terms

**Term**:
{One or two sentences stating what it is, not what it does.}
_Avoid_: synonyms the organization does not use separated by commas

**Term**:
{One or two sentences stating what it is, not what it does.}
_Avoid_: synonyms the organization does not use separated by commas
```

## Rules

- **Be opinionated.** When multiple words exist for the same concept, pick the best one and list the others under `_Avoid_`.
- **Keep definitions tight.** One or two sentences max. Define what it IS, not what it does.
- **Only terms specific to internal audit or this organization.** General English and general business terms stay out.
- **Only settled definitions.** Keep unresolved terminology questions in the conversation until a definition is settled. Task-specific exceptions and temporary interpretations stay in the conversation.
- **Definitions belong here.** Audit methodology belongs in `METHODOLOGY.md`, organizational facts in `ORGANIZATION.md`, and engagement material under `engagements/`.
