# Organizational Context

Maintain a compact overview in `ORGANIZATION.md` that is useful across engagements. Create it from [ORGANIZATION-TEMPLATE.md](ORGANIZATION-TEMPLATE.md).

## Content and evidence

Capture supported facts about purpose and objectives, operating model, major activities, governance, and significant systems or changes. Detailed process and control inventories, engagement scope, and findings belong under `engagements/`; include only a concise shared fact when it has clear value across audits. Add headings as the facts warrant them.

Use supplied organizational material or explicit user statements. Keep lightweight references beside related facts. For a user-provided fact, record that basis and the date; include an effective date or period when the fact depends on time. Keep inferences and temporary engagement assumptions out of the shared facts.

## Maintenance

Clarify conflicting facts before replacing settled context. Update changing facts in place with their current basis and effective period. Keep definitions in the glossary and use its terms here.
