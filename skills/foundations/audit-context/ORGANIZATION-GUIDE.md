# Organizational Context

Maintain a compact overview in `ORGANIZATION.md` that is useful across engagements.

## Content and evidence

Capture supported facts about purpose and objectives, operating model, major activities, governance, and significant systems or changes. Detailed process and control inventories, engagement scope, and findings belong under `engagements/`; include only a concise shared fact when it has clear value across audits.

Use supplied organizational material or explicit user statements. Keep lightweight references beside related facts. For a user-provided fact, record that basis and the date; include an effective date or period when the fact depends on time. Keep inferences and temporary engagement assumptions out of the shared facts.

## Starting structure

Read [ORGANIZATION-TEMPLATE.md](ORGANIZATION-TEMPLATE.md) when creating the document. During explicit workspace setup, copy it even if organizational material is unavailable. Otherwise, use its structure as supported content becomes available. The prompts describe information to gather; they are not facts about the organization.

Replace prompts with supported content, adapt headings to the organization, and remove unused sections when they no longer help. Keep the status clear when only part of the overview is populated.

## Maintenance

Clarify conflicting facts before replacing settled context. Update changing facts in place with their current basis and effective period. Retain only material open questions that would affect future audit work; remove them when resolved. Keep definitions in the working glossary and use its terms here.
