# Internal Audit Glossary

## Language

**Risk:**
The possibility that an event may affect the achievement of an objective.

**Objective:**
A result an organization, function, process, or activity seeks to achieve.

**Event:**
An uncertain occurrence or set of circumstances that could affect the achievement of an objective.

**Cause:**
A circumstance or condition that could give rise to or contribute to an event.

**Consequence:**
An outcome or effect that could result from an event and affect the achievement of an objective.
